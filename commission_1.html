Commission an artwork

Have a person, a place or a moment you want kept? I make commissions by hand on handmade paper.

  1. Send a reference. Attach a photo and tell me what it means to you.
  2. Agree the details. Size and price are agreed by email before I begin. A deposit is paid first.
  3. I make it. A medium piece takes about a day, a detailed one around three. Delivery usually takes 10 to 15 days.

Commissions follow the commission policy.
