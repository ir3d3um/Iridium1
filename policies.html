One-of-a-kind originals

Every piece is an original, made by hand, and there is only one of each. Once a piece is sold it cannot be bought again, and it is marked Sold on the site. Colours may look slightly different on your screen than on the paper.

Prices

The price of each artwork is fixed and is set in Indian rupees (INR). It is the same for every buyer in every country. Amounts shown in other currencies are approximate guides only, worked out from a daily exchange rate, and the rupee price is the price that applies. Shipping and import duties are not included in the artwork price (see below).

How buying works

  1. Send an enquiry about the artwork using the Enquire button or the contact page.
  2. I reply by email with the artwork price, shipping cost to your country and payment details.
  3. Nothing is final until you confirm and payment is received. The piece is reserved for you once I confirm your order by email.
  4. I pack and ship your piece and send you tracking details.

Welcome discount

Create an account and verify your email to receive a personal welcome code for 5% off the artwork price of your first order. Each verified email address can use the code once. It applies to the artwork price only (not shipping or duties), and cannot be combined with other offers. I may confirm your verified email when you order.

Shipping and delivery

I ship worldwide from India. Each piece is packed flat and protected, and sent by a tracked, insured courier. I photograph every piece and its packing before it leaves me.

Shipping cost depends on the size and weight of the piece and on the country it is going to. I quote it by email before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Delivery usually takes 10 to 15 days from dispatch, depending on the destination. This is an estimate and is confirmed when you order. Customs checks and courier delays can sometimes add time.

Customs, duties and taxes

Import duties, customs fees and taxes charged by your country are the buyer’s responsibility, and they are not included in the artwork price or the shipping cost. They vary by country, so please check what applies where you live. If a parcel is refused or left uncollected, the buyer is responsible for the costs of returning it.

Damage and returns

Because every piece is an original, I do not offer exchanges, and I cannot accept returns for change of mind, a different expectation, or colour differences between screen and paper. I accept a return only if the piece arrives damaged.

  • Tell me quickly. Email me within 48 hours of delivery that the piece is damaged. Please include clear photos of the damage and of the packaging, and, if you can, a short video of opening the parcel. Keep all the packaging.
  • Return window. The return must be agreed and the piece sent back within 10 to 15 days of you receiving it. Please wait for my confirmation before sending anything back.
  • Return before refund. The damaged piece must be returned to me, well packed and tracked. I issue the refund or credit only after I have received the piece and checked it against the photos I took before shipping.
  • Your choice of remedy. If your claim is accepted, you can choose a refund of the artwork price to your original payment method, or store credit for another piece. Because each piece is unique, a replacement of the same work is not possible.
  • Return shipping. If damage in transit is confirmed, I cover the cost of the return.
  • My decision. I assess each claim carefully and fairly, and my decision on whether a piece was damaged in transit is final, subject to your legal rights where you live.

Commissions

For a commission, the size, subject and price are agreed by email before I begin. A deposit is paid first and the balance before shipping. Commissioned pieces are made specially for you, so they cannot be returned or exchanged, except for damage in transit as described above. The number of revisions is agreed when we settle the details.

Copyright

When you buy a piece you own the physical artwork. I keep the copyright in the artwork. You may not copy, photograph for sale, print, reproduce or use it commercially without my written permission. All images on this site are protected by copyright.

Privacy

I use your name, email and delivery details only to answer your enquiry, process your order and run your account, and I never sell or share them, except with the courier and payment provider where needed to complete your order. If you make an account, your email, saved artworks and enquiries are stored with a service called Firebase. To have your details removed, send a message through the contact form.

These policies are a plain-language guide. Consumer rules differ between countries, and nothing here limits rights you have by law where you live. Questions? Use the contact form.
