About ir3d3um

Art should connect.

I am Iridium, a self-taught artist based in India, and my journey began in the most unexpected of times.

During the pandemic, when the world slowed down, something in me woke up. I had always carried a quiet love for artistic design. With more time on my hands, I found myself deep in Instagram and Pinterest, watching artists create work that left me breathless. One day, I decided to stop watching and start trying.

What surprised me most was that I began to go beyond what I was seeing. I started experimenting, taking risks, and finding a voice that was entirely my own.

My work is rooted in the textures and traditions of India. I work primarily on handmade paper, using watercolours, acrylics, and pen and ink. For certain pieces, I use organic pigments made from raw earth, minerals, and processed flowers.

At the heart of everything I create is a simple belief: art should connect. When a piece finds its way into someone’s home and they write to tell me how it makes them feel, that is what keeps me going.

ir3d3um is an extension of my values, my curiosity, and my joy. I hope something here speaks to you too.

— Iridium

Commission a drawing

Have a person, a place or a moment you want kept? I draw commissions by hand in pen and ink on handmade paper.

  1. Send a reference. A photo, and a few lines on what it means to you.
  2. Agree the details. Size and price are settled before I begin, and both depend on how much detail the drawing needs.
  3. I draw it. A medium piece takes about a day to draw. A detailed one takes around three.
